---
title: The interface layer is where AI becomes real
description: Models matter. But the product layer determines whether intelligence becomes useful, trusted, and habit-forming.
date: 2026-08-09
tags: [ai, interfaces, product]
---

The frontier model is not the product. It is the engine.

The product is the set of choices that turns raw capability into something a person can understand, trust, and return to. Latency, memory, voice, embodiment, permissions, error recovery, emotional tone, and context all become part of the experience.

The next important AI companies will not simply expose better models. They will design better relationships between humans and machines.
