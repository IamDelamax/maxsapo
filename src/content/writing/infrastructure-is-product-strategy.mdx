---
title: AI infrastructure is product strategy
description: In AI, compute cost, latency, and reliability are not backend details. They define the product surface.
date: 2026-08-09
tags: [ai, infrastructure, strategy]
---

In traditional software, infrastructure was often hidden behind the product. In AI, it increasingly determines the product.

If inference is too expensive, the feature does not scale. If latency is too high, the interface feels broken. If reliability is uneven, trust collapses. If the developer experience is painful, the ecosystem routes around you.

The strategic product questions now include hardware, serving, model architecture, cost curves, and tooling. The stack got deeper. Product work did too.
